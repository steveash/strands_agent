## Why this matters for understanding Strands

This makes the Strands-based operator loop easier to inspect before reopening a session:
- tool-heavy sessions now show whether they are failing because of tests, generic tool failures, or both,
- intervention-heavy sessions now show whether the queue is dominated by test approvals, edit approvals, or other tooling.

That is useful because Strands is most interesting here when tool use, steering, and approvals are visible as system behavior rather than hidden behind chat output.
