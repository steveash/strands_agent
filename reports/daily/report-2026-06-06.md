## B) Why this step matters for understanding Strands
This repo is teaching Strands through visible control surfaces: approvals, continuations, session recovery, and triage. The intervention filter is where those approval-driven state transitions become legible. By pinning exact intervention-mix strings in smoke coverage, the prototype now protects one of the most Strands-specific seams: what happened before/after approval, what kind of target was involved, and whether the agent resumed with a continuation.

## C) What Steve will learn from it

- How to turn a complex TUI/agent interaction into a stable public smoke contract.
- How the picker and in-app switcher differ: same underlying triage model, different UI path, both now verified against explicit intervention output.
