## B) Why this step matters for understanding Strands

This repo is supposed to make the agent loop visible.

Today’s work helps in two ways:
- the **timeline smoke** makes runtime and persistence behavior inspectable without launching the TUI, which sharpens the mental model around “what happened in the Strands loop” vs “what got saved”; and
- the **smoke-doc artifact outputs** make repo maintenance observable too, so wrapper/help/README drift can be audited as structured artifacts instead of vague terminal output.
