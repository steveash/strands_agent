## B) Why this step matters for understanding Strands

A big part of learning Strands in this repo is understanding that the agent loop continues across approval boundaries.

The live timeline already showed that clearly, but the saved-session triage surfaces lagged behind. Aligning the reopen flows with the live timeline matters because those surfaces are how Steve inspects resumed agent work, approval queues, and interrupted sessions after the fact.

This makes the Strands control flow easier to see as one coherent loop instead of separate live-vs-saved mental models.
