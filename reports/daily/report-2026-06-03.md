## B) Why this step matters for understanding Strands

This prototype is meant to make the Strands loop visible.

Approvals are not just a yes/no gate. They create queue state, resume tool execution, and then synthesize a follow-up prompt back into the agent loop. By making the target and continuation step explicit, the prototype exposes more of the real control flow Steve needs to understand.

## C) What Steve will learn from it

Steve should get a clearer feel for:
- how approval-backed work can be normalized across fake and live runtimes,
- how a resumed tool execution differs from the synthetic continuation prompt that follows it,
- how compact timeline summaries can encode meaningful agent-loop state without dumping raw dicts,
- and how to test steering/intervention seams deterministically.
